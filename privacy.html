Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how PSP Classic (“the App”) handles information. PSP Classic is a PlayStation Portable (PSP) emulator for Android based on PPSSPP and released under the GNU GPL.

1No account

The App does not require you to create an account. You can use it without signing in.

2Data stored on your device

Game files, save data, settings, control layouts, and downloaded games are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a user account system and do not collect this content as a cloud profile.

3Advertising (AdMob)

The App may show ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and process certain information to provide and measure ads, such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, ad interaction data, and diagnostic data, according to Google’s own policies. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your device settings and applicable consent rules.

You can reduce ads in the App with the “Hide ads” option in Settings, where available. You can also reset or limit advertising ID use in your Android system settings.

4Remote game catalog

To show downloadable games, the App may download a remote configuration file (for example from a URL you or the developer configure). That request may include standard technical data such as IP address and user agent. The catalog content itself is not a personal profile of you.

5Network permission

The App needs internet access to load ads, fetch the remote game catalog, and open links you tap (such as Telegram, YouTube, privacy policy, or store pages).

6Third-party services and links

Links to Telegram, YouTube, GitHub, Google Play, or other sites are provided for convenience. Those services have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their practices.

7Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service linked from the App, contact that service’s provider.

8Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after changes means you should review the revised policy.
